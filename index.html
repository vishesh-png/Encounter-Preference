Definitions. Calls done = COMPLETED/RECONSULTED appointments (Screening Call offline/online; Repeat = Follow Up, Patient Queries, Report Reading). Prescribed = the call's encounter carries at least one order of the tab's kind (meds = drug Rx line, tests = lab test order, therapy = therapy Rx). Converted = paid invoice with a drug / lab item on that encounter; therapy = therapy order consumed or therapy invoice paid. Not-prescribed reasons come from the doctor's form answer on the encounter. Not-converted reasons are the patient's opt-out reason from encounter preferences (drug / lab-test / therapy level, falling back to the encounter-level opt-out); where no opt-out is logged, the billing funnel is shown (med serviceability, invoice raised-but-unpaid, no invoice). Not prescribed % is over calls done; Not converted % is over prescribed calls. Diagnosis = patient's latest merged-rx paperform diagnosis (MH / STI / ED+ / PE+ / ED+PE+ / Others). Current week is partial.
